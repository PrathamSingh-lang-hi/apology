I’m Truly Sorry

I don’t have any excuses. I just want to say that I’m really sorry for hurting you. I know I made a mistake, and I will make it right. Please forgive me and give me another chance to prove my love to you.

Maafi mangta hoon agar mera dil thoda bhatak gaya tha, Par aapke bina mera har ek pal kahin atak gaya tha. Ab chodo bhi ye naraazgi, phir se muskura do, Mere is khali jahan ko apne prem se saja do. Hamara ye saath wahi pavitra silsila hai, Jaise Radha-Krishna ko unka adhura ishq mila hai.
